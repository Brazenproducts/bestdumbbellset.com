Bowflex SelectTech 552 vs NordicTrack Select-A-Weight

The two most popular adjustable dumbbell sets go head-to-head. Here's which one actually wins for your home gym.

Quick Verdict

🏆 Winner: Bowflex SelectTech 552

The Bowflex wins on weight range, adjustment speed, and long-term reliability. The NordicTrack is excellent but slightly bulkier and slower to adjust.

Bowflex SelectTech 552 — Strengths

  • Dual-dial adjustment is the fastest on the market
  • Goes down to 5 lbs — better for warm-ups and rehab
  • Compact, well-balanced design
  • Proven track record with millions sold

NordicTrack Select-A-Weight — Strengths

  • Consistent 2.5 lb increments throughout entire range
  • Includes tray — no extra purchase needed
  • Goes up to 55 lbs vs 52.5 lbs for Bowflex
  • Slightly more affordable

Which Should You Buy?

If speed of adjustment is your priority and you want the most proven option, go Bowflex. If consistent 2.5-lb increments through the full range matter most and you want the tray included, NordicTrack is a strong choice.
